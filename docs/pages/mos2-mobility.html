0. Summary

On one and the same host and Lanczos chain (2026-08-20 144b22 ns24 sectors, 12×12 coarse mesh × 22 spinor bands, relaxed defects, vacuum-aligned $\Delta V$, 4 symmetry sectors), this page compares four levels of scattering theory — {full $T$-matrix, Born} × {rest self-energy $\Sigma$ kept, dropped} — giving defect-limited mobilities for three defects × electrons/holes (all 12 cells of the matrix delivered), with K-valley zoom spectral functions and the vertex’s Wannier decay providing vector-level evidence for every conclusion. Three headlines: (i) Born is wrong by 1–2 orders of magnitude for deep wells (V$_\mathrm{S}$ holes 53×, strict second order 190×) — the same mechanism as the PdCoO$_2$ vacancy; (ii) $\Sigma$’s role flips with the defect: screening for V$_\mathrm{S}$ (19.9→13.3 eV), building a CBM resonance for O$_\mathrm{S}$ ($\Sigma$(CBM)=42 eV, 4× the bare vertex; dropping $\Sigma$ inflates $\mu_e$ by a spurious 72×), and near-cancellation for Se$_\mathrm{S}$ (3.5 vs 3.12 eV, 0.3–0.6 eV residual); (iii) of the four levels, only full $T$ + $\Sigma$ is simultaneously consistent for all three defects and both carriers. Carrier convention: the sheet density is fixed at $n=10^{10}$ cm$^{-2}$ and $E_F(T)$ is obtained by bisection on the pristine primitive-cell bands; $\mu\propto 1/n_d$. The downfolding now has a ground-truth validation (§4b): run on a 6×6 coarse mesh the chain reproduces the periodic array, i.e. the same system a 6×6 SOC supercell diagonalises directly, and after removing one $\Delta V$-alignment constant per defect (V$_\mathrm{S}$ +17.3, O$_\mathrm{S}$ −25.9 meV — the same number in the valence manifold, the gap and the conduction manifold to ≤0.7 meV, and cancelling in every energy difference) it matches that supercell across the whole 2.7 eV window: deep-level positions to <1 meV, splittings to 0.0 meV, spectral weights to 1.9%. Chain depth and SVD compression contribute ≤1 meV.

The $\Sigma$ colour maps of §3 suggest Se$_\mathrm{S}$ is the weakest scatterer on both sides, yet $\mu_h$(Se$_\mathrm{S}$) = 4303 is 3.5× below $\mu_h$(V$_\mathrm{S}$) = 15243. And the on-shell trace? A fair follow-up is that the tables above evaluate all 22 bands at one fixed $\omega$, so 21 of the 22 terms are off shell — what if instead each band is taken at its own quasiparticle energy, $\sum_n\Sigma_{nn}(k,E_{nk})$? Computing that at K (22 separate on-shell evaluations, $R_c$=5a) gives on-shell traces $\sum_n\Gamma_n$ = 20.39 (V$_\mathrm{S}$), 28.84 (O$_\mathrm{S}$), 11.38 meV (Se$_\mathrm{S}$), of which the carrier's own band holds 0.16% / 0.57% / 11.5% at the VBM and 0.79% / 17.6% / 1.4% at the CBM. So the on-shell trace fails to track transport too — it compresses a 40× spread in the VBM channel (Se$_\mathrm{S}$ 1.313 vs V$_\mathrm{S}$ 0.032 meV) into a 1.8× spread and puts V$_\mathrm{S}$ above Se$_\mathrm{S}$ — because it is still dominated by bands the carrier never occupies (V$_\mathrm{S}$: bands 15–16 at $-4.6$ eV, 2.3–2.7 meV, and 31–32 at $+3.4$ eV, 2.9–3.2 meV). Two remarks. First, this quantity is not the trace of any matrix: its terms are evaluated at 22 different arguments, so unlike $\mathrm{Tr}\,\Sigma(k,\omega)$ it has no basis-free meaning — it is well defined only in the band eigenbasis, which $H_k$ singles out. It becomes a genuine invariant only inside a degenerate manifold (the K-point conduction spin pair, the doubly degenerate $\Gamma$ valence maximum), where it is the trace of the decay-rate matrix and equals the sum of the eigenmode widths. Second, the physically natural band sum is over a constant-energy surface, not over bands at fixed $k$: $\sum_{nk}\Gamma_{nk}\delta(E-E_{nk})$ is the total out-scattering rate at energy $E$, tied by the optical theorem to the defect's total cross-section and by its energy integral to the Friedel sum rule — and that is exactly what the $\Gamma(\varepsilon)$ figures below plot. Useful validation: Se$_\mathrm{S}$'s band-26 on-shell width here, 1.313 meV, matches the independent 300×300 grid median at the edge, 1.334 meV. Script diag/onshell_trace.py (Anvil job 20142527).

Kubo versus Boltzmann. A fair objection is that the Kubo formula needs the complete trace, not one band diagonal. It does — but a different trace: $\sigma_{xx}=\frac{\pi\hbar e^2}{\Omega}\int d\omega\,(-\partial_\omega f)\,\mathrm{Tr}[v_x A(\omega)v_x A(\omega)]$ over the full matrix spectral function $A=\frac{i}{2\pi}(G^R-G^A)$, which carries two projections that $\mathrm{Tr}\,\Sigma$ has neither of: the velocity vertices weight each band pair by $|v_{mn}|^2$, and the spectral functions put the weight on shell — a band $\Delta E$ off shell enters with $A_{nn}(\omega)\simeq\Gamma/2\pi\Delta E^2$ instead of $2/\pi\Gamma$. For the bands carrying V$_\mathrm{S}$'s excess trace, $\Delta E$ = 3–7 eV against $\hbar\Gamma\sim0.3$ meV, so the leading interband-coherence correction is $O((\Gamma/\Delta E)^2)\sim10^{-8}$, and the 9–26× trace differences are invisible to any transport formula. Measured (KG bubble on the 300×300 grid, $\eta$=2.5 meV, four levels differing only in what is kept of $\Sigma$ and $v$; Anvil jobs 20149383/84/85). The implementation is validated by a level that keeps both $\Sigma$ and $v$ band-diagonal, which must analytically reproduce SERTA broadened to $\gamma=\Gamma/2+\eta$; it lands within 1.2–7.8% of that reference across all six cases below (the reference is a model Lorentzian built from the on-shell $\Gamma$, so it ignores the Re$\,\Sigma$ shift and the $\omega$-dependence of $\Sigma$ — the largest deviation is O$_\mathrm{S}$ electrons, precisely where the CBM resonance makes $\Sigma(\omega)$ vary fastest and the model is least applicable; that the interband and coherence ratios are identical across all six cases shows this is a limitation of the reference, not of the implementation). Every run gates on hermiticity and non-negative diagonal weight, and all pass (0.0, $+0.8\ldots1.3\times10^{-4}$).

Why $\mu_\mathrm{KG}$ is far below $\mu_\mathrm{SERTA}$, and why that is not a physics statement. Against SERTA with $\tau=\hbar/\Gamma$ the bubble gives ratios of 0.018–0.30 — but that gap is entirely the artificial broadening, not Kubo-versus-Boltzmann: in the bubble the Lorentzian half-width is $\Gamma/2+\eta$, and substituting $\tau=\hbar/(\Gamma+2\eta)$ into the SERTA sum reproduces the bubble to a few percent (e.g. 235.3 vs 239.2 for V$_\mathrm{S}$ holes, 228.1 vs 223.3 for O$_\mathrm{S}$ holes). The reason $\eta$ wins is the dilute limit: at $n_d=10^{12}$ cm$^{-2}$ the physical $\hbar\Gamma$ is only 0.03–4 meV, so 100% of the 300 K thermal weight, for every defect and both carriers, has $\Gamma<2\eta$. A meaningful bubble would need $\eta\lesssim0.1\,\Gamma\sim5\,\mu$eV with an $\omega$ grid finer still — and in that limit $A$ becomes $\delta$-like and the bubble reduces analytically to SERTA. In the dilute limit the two are therefore not competing numerical schemes; the latter is the analytic solution of the former, which is exactly why the production engine runs an NW ladder at $\eta=600/\mathrm{NW}$ meV and extrapolates linearly in $1/\mathrm{NW}$.

Record of a correction. An earlier revision of this page quoted a coherence correction of 0.12% and a large "interband velocity" enhancement (23×) from this same calculation. Both were wrong: the code built the spectral matrix as the element-wise imaginary part $A=-\mathrm{Im}\,G/\pi$ instead of the anti-Hermitian part $A=\frac{i}{2\pi}(G-G^\dagger)$. Those agree only for complex-symmetric $G$, which $(z-H-\Sigma)^{-1}$ is not when $\Sigma=cN_kT_{kk}$ is non-symmetric; the element-wise form is not Hermitian, admits negative spectral weight, and — decisively — does not transform covariantly under the rotation to the band basis, so taking a band-diagonal of it is meaningless. In a toy case it shifts $\mathrm{Tr}[vAvA]$ by a factor $-10$. It was caught only by adding the band-diagonal-$v$ validation level above, which came out exactly 2× off its analytic target. The fixed code now gates on hermiticity ($\le10^{-12}$) and non-negative diagonal weight at every run; both pass (0.0 and $+1.1\times10^{-4}$).

What the KG bubble cannot deliver here, and why. The absolute ratio $\mu_\mathrm{KG}/\mu_\mathrm{SERTA}$ is not reportable at a numerically accessible $\eta$, and the diagnosis is worth recording. In the KG spectral function the Lorentzian half-width is $\Gamma/2+\eta$, so the artificial broadening competes with the physical linewidth — and at $n_d=10^{12}$ cm$^{-2}$ the physical $\hbar\Gamma$ is only 0.03–4 meV, so at $\eta$=2.5 meV 100% of the thermal weight, for every defect and both carriers, satisfies $\Gamma<2\eta$. The consequences are visible in the numbers: the band-diagonal Lorentzian reference becomes nearly defect-independent (220 / 229 / 236 cm²/Vs for the three defects' holes, 328 / 428 / 431 for electrons), $\mu_\mathrm{KG}$ likewise (2592 / 2615 / 2845 and 1429 / 1761 / 1778), and their ratio is a universal 11.7 (holes) / 4.2 (electrons) across three scatterers as different as a 13 eV vacancy well, a CBM-resonant substitutional and an isovalent one. A number that does not distinguish those three is not measuring them — it is measuring $\eta$. A meaningful evaluation would need $\eta\lesssim0.1\,\Gamma\sim5\,\mu$eV and an $\omega$ grid finer still, some 200× the cost; but in that limit $A$ becomes $\delta$-like and the bubble reduces analytically to SERTA plus interband terms suppressed by $(\gamma/\Delta E)^2$. In the dilute limit KG-bubble and SERTA are therefore not two comparable numerical schemes — the latter is the analytic solution of the former, which is precisely why the production engine runs an NW ladder at $\eta=600/\mathrm{NW}$ meV and extrapolates linearly in $1/\mathrm{NW}$ instead of evaluating a bubble. 2b. The three rates: quasiparticle, momentum relaxation, full IBTE (2026-08-25)

The same 300×300 grid, now carrying the whole ladder the mobility engine solves internally, state by state: the quasiparticle rate $1/\tau_\mathrm{qp}=\Gamma_\mathrm{qp}/\hbar$ (what SERTA uses), the momentum-relaxation rate $1/\tau_\mathrm{tr}=\sum_{s'}P_{ss'}(1-\cos\theta_{ss'})$, and the full linearized-BTE rate $1/\tau_\mathrm{IBTE}=|v|^2/(F\!\cdot\!v)$ with $F=\tau_\mathrm{qp}v+\tau_\mathrm{qp}PF$. The kernel $P_{ss'}=\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}c\frac{N_k^2}{N_\mathrm{grid}^2}|T_{ss'}|^2L_\eta(\varepsilon_s-\varepsilon_{s'})$ is built from the same $R_c$=5a image-free $T$, with the optical theorem $\sum_{s'}P_{ss'}=\Gamma_s/\hbar$ enforced (median residual 0.2–0.5%).

This is the vertex correction resolved state by state. For Se$_\mathrm{S}$ holes the three separate strongly below the $\Gamma$-pocket edge — window medians 0.740 → 0.300 → 0.247 ps$^{-1}$, a factor 3 — the signature of an isovalent weak perturbation scattering into small angles, where $(1-\cos\theta)$ removes most of the quasiparticle rate. V$_\mathrm{S}$ and O$_\mathrm{S}$ stay nearly degenerate (1.844/1.750/1.710 and 1.394/1.561/1.482 ps$^{-1}$): deep wells scatter near-isotropically, and O$_\mathrm{S}$ holes are even slightly backscattering-dominant ($1/\tau_\mathrm{tr}>1/\tau_\mathrm{qp}$). At the band edges themselves all three converge for every defect, because there only K/K′ intervalley processes survive and those carry $\langle\cos\theta\rangle\approx0$ — no forward-scattering suppression is available. The wide spread of the IBTE points is physical, not numerical: $\tau_\mathrm{IBTE}$ is direction-dependent around the trigonally warped constant-energy contours, whereas $\tau_\mathrm{qp}$ and $\tau_\mathrm{tr}$ are scalars per state.

At fixed $n_\mathrm{lancz}$=16, tightening svd_tol from $10^{-4}$ to $10^{-10}$ — which takes the sector ranks from 179/89 to the full 264/132, i.e. no starting-block compression at all — moves the doublet by −0.5 / +0.5 meV, and moves the operator itself by even less: the label-independent $\sum_\mathrm{sectors}R_0^\dagger R_0$ agrees to $7.4\times10^{-8}$ relative, so the 85 discarded SVD directions per sector really do carry nothing. Going from the 16-deep Banff chain to the 24-deep Anvil one moves it by −1.0 / −0.5 meV — that step changes the binary and the machine as well as the depth, so it bounds the two together rather than isolating depth.

The two are separable, though, because the front end is checked to be the same calculation: $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf{k}}$, $V_{AA}$ and the sector partition $(u_k,u_b)$ are fixed before any Lanczos iteration or SVD gauge choice, and across all three chains — Banff v37 and Anvil r39p, two machines, two MKL builds — they are bitwise identical (max|d| = 0.000). The retained subspaces match too: $R_0^\dagger R_0$ is gauge-invariant and identifies the subspace, and the four sector subspaces agree as a set to $7.8\times10^{-7}$ relative, which is all $\Sigma_\mathrm{rest}=\sum_\mathrm{sectors}R_0^\dagger[z-T]^{-1}R_0/N_k$ ever uses. So the ≤1 meV really is chain depth, and edt_r39p on Anvil and edt_v37_nockpt on Banff are the same code along the sectors path. (This front-end check is V$_\mathrm{S}$-only: O$_\mathrm{S}$’s one older 6×6 chain used nbndsub=10 and a −6.90 eV window, a different active space entirely, so there is nothing to compare it against.) One trap surfaced here: the sector file index is not a stable label. Sectors 1 and 2 are a Kramers partner pair and their labels come out swapped between runs — not only across the two binaries but between the two Banff runs as well, presumably from the LAPACK ordering of a degenerate pair in the symmetry analysis. The residual is one constant per defect, and that is a $\Delta V$ alignment convention. A constant $\delta$ added to $\Delta V$ adds $\delta$ times the identity to the defect cell’s Hamiltonian, shifting every level of that cell rigidly, while the host VBM — read from the unperturbed $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf{k}}$ — does not move. The chain fixes its $\Delta V$ zero in the vacuum (pot_align='vacuum'), the supercell truth fixes its own by the deep-state alignment above; a mismatch between the two conventions must therefore appear as exactly one rigid, defect-specific constant. That is falsifiable for free, because a constant has to shift the whole spectrum and not just the in-gap levels. So the downfolding itself is not carrying a 20 meV error: after one constant per defect, the 6×6 downfolded DOS reproduces the 6×6 SOC supercell across the entire 2.7 eV window — every valence and conduction manifold peak, the deep doublet’s position to <1 meV, its splitting to 0.0 meV, and its spectral weight to 1.9%. Because this constant shifts host and defect levels together it cancels in every energy difference, which is what the scattering rates and mobilities of §§1–2 are built from.

The rigid-constant test has teeth — and it also picks the geometry. A fit that always succeeds proves nothing, so it is worth knowing what a wrong pairing looks like. The $\Delta V$ cubes were generated from the relaxed defect supercells, so sc_rlx is the truth used above; repeating the whole comparison against the ideal-geometry supercells (sc, same pristine reference, same robust alignment) is a control in which the chain and the truth describe slightly different physical systems. The window-to-window spread of the fitted constant then grows from 0.58 to 4.75 meV for V$_\mathrm{S}$ and from 0.95 to 15.5 meV for O$_\mathrm{S}$ (valence −17.49, conduction −32.96, whole window −26.73 meV — no single number fits), and the fraction of the discrepancy a best-fit constant fails to remove goes from 0.048 to 0.698 in the V$_\mathrm{S}$ valence manifold and from 0.082 to 0.935 in the O$_\mathrm{S}$ conduction manifold, where removing a constant now barely helps at all. So the test discriminates, its success on the relaxed data is meaningful rather than automatic, and it independently confirms that the relaxed supercells are the right pairing for relaxed cubes. 5. Method and the verification chain

  • Inputs: the 2026-08-20 MODE C chain (144b22 ns24 sectors; NLr 459/459/230/230, herm(A) ~5e-15) plus a w90 rerun on that chain’s own NSCF ($\Omega$=37.519 Ų, agreeing with an independent wannierization to 5 digits) — the u.mat must come from the same NSCF as the chain (gauge continuity).
  • Engine: the $\omega$-resolved vertex is assembled directly in the chain’s Bloch basis (the support basis comes from a Bloch Gram matrix, avoiding an einsum rotation, ~9 s per bin; gT gate: 1.9e-11 element by element against the g8-verified Wannier einsum route); host $g_S$ on a dense 480² spectrum binned at 1.5 meV; a matched-$\eta$ Lorentzian kernel plus optical-theorem enforcement (2 rounds of row scaling, median 0.4–0.8%); and a damped IBTE with the on-shell null mode removed (spectral radius <1, direct and iterative agreeing to ~2e-4).
  • Born variants: a golden-rule kernel $P=|\langle u|\tilde V|u'\rangle|^2$ with $\gamma\equiv\hbar\sum P$ ($s\equiv1$ by construction) — a consistent $O(V^2)$ definition that avoids the mixed orders of $|V+VG_0V|^2$; in the $-\Sigma$ variants the vertex is $\omega$-independent, so the continued fraction is skipped.
  • Gate list: g1 chain eps≡xml (0.0); g2/g2b hr/u.mat ≤0.03 meV; gE (bit-identical, 0.0); gT 1.9e-11; g4 support residual ~1e-6; g8 support vs a full-dimension 3168² direct solve ≤6e-4 (= vertex truncation × $T$ resonance enhancement); g5 optical theorem; g6 IBTE; g7 isotropy; wdecay all LOCALIZED; and Im $T_{nn}\le0$ over the whole mesh.
